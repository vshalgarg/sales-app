Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Hisabio

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Codemonks ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Hisabio mobile application ("App"). Hisabio is designed to help users manage business records, including customer and supplier information, transactions, balances, and other related financial records.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Hisabio.

1. Information We Collect

User Account Information

Hisabio users are created and managed by an authorized administrator. We may collect and store the following information for user accounts:

  • Name
  • Username or other account identification information
  • Password or authentication credentials

Hisabio does not require users to provide their mobile phone number for account registration or login.

Customer and Supplier Information

Users may enter and manage information relating to their customers and suppliers within the App. Depending on the information entered by the user, this may include:

  • Names
  • Contact information
  • Addresses
  • Business-related information
  • Account and ledger details
  • Transaction records
  • Outstanding balances
  • Payment-related records
  • Other information voluntarily entered into the App

This information is provided and managed by authorized users of Hisabio and is stored in our database to provide the App's core functionality.

Files and Documents

Hisabio may access files stored on your device when you choose to upload, attach, or use a file within the App. The App accesses only the files necessary to provide the requested feature or functionality. We do not access or collect files for purposes unrelated to the App's functionality.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

We use crash analytics and diagnostic tools to identify, analyze, and resolve technical issues and improve the performance and stability of the App.

Crash analytics may collect technical information such as:

  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Information about how the App was functioning when an error occurred

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected through Hisabio to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate authorized users
  • Provide customer and supplier management features
  • Maintain ledger and transaction records
  • Display account balances and business information
  • Store and retrieve information entered by authorized users
  • Enable users to upload and access relevant files and documents
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the App
  • Identify and resolve technical problems
  • Monitor and improve App performance and stability
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services

3. Data Storage

Information entered into Hisabio is stored in our database to enable the App's functionality and allow authorized users to access their business records.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

Codemonks does not sell users' personal information.

We do not share customer, supplier, ledger, or transaction information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may use third-party service providers, including crash analytics providers, to help us monitor App performance and identify technical issues. These service providers may process limited technical or diagnostic information as necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request.

5. File Access

Hisabio may request permission to access files or media stored on your device when required for a feature you choose to use.

Files are accessed only for the functionality requested by the user. Users can manage or revoke applicable permissions through their device settings, subject to the impact that revoking such permissions may have on certain App features.

6. Data Retention

We retain user account information and business-related information for as long as necessary to provide the Hisabio services or until the information is deleted by an authorized administrator, subject to any applicable legal or operational requirements.

Customer, supplier, ledger, transaction, and other business information entered into the App may remain stored until deleted by an authorized administrator or otherwise removed according to our applicable data management procedures.

7. Account and Data Deletion

User accounts and associated data may be deleted by an authorized administrator.

If you are unable to request deletion through your administrator or wish to submit a data deletion request, you may contact us at:

Data Deletion Email
crm@codemonks.in

Upon receiving a valid and authorized deletion request, we will process the request in accordance with applicable laws and our data retention obligations.

8. User Responsibilities

Hisabio allows authorized users to enter and manage information about customers, suppliers, and other individuals.

Users and organizations using Hisabio are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary authority, permissions, and legal basis to collect, enter, store, and manage such information using the App.

9. Children's Privacy

Hisabio is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been improperly submitted to Hisabio, please contact us at crm@codemonks.in .

10. Third-Party Services

Hisabio may use third-party services to support App functionality, including crash reporting and analytics services.

These third-party services may collect and process certain technical or diagnostic information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements.

Any changes will be posted within the App or through an updated version of this Privacy Policy. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will indicate when it was most recently revised.
